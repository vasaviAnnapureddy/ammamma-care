2. The root problem (brainstorming result)
What residents say: "The government never gives us money. We cannot go to offices."
What they feel: Hopeless, ignored, afraid of being cheated or asked for bribes.
The real root: Nobody carries each resident's case from start to finish with proof. Applications have no reference number, nobody follows up, nobody escalates when an office goes silent, and missing documents block everything quietly.
Locked root problem: Elderly residents who are entitled to money and care lose it because no one owns their case end to end, keeps proof, and pushes it up the free escalation channels that already exist.
The important discovery: the system already has free doors that reach a real officer. Most people just do not use them, or give up after one try.
3. The doorstep path (checked facts)

The honest truth: nobody can guarantee the money. But this path removes almost every office visit, keeps proof at every step, and uses channels that make an officer answer in writing. That is the best real chance.
Other help in the same tool

Ayushman Vay Vandana (70+): Telangana rollout unclear, so the tool marks it "check locally", not "eligible".
What will NOT happen (be honest with residents)
Guaranteed pension money. We can only push and prove.
Pension money inside 10 days. Hackathon proof will be applications, acknowledgement numbers, grievance IDs and accounts opened, not money yet.
Government officers bringing pensions home without any account or application.
Printing at the home.
4. What we are building
An AI case manager for caretakers. Residents never use it. Two staff members do.
Resident profile: name, age, gender, language, documents as yes or no (Aadhaar, bank or post account, ration card, income certificate, age proof), current pension yes or no, health needs. Never store Aadhaar or bank numbers.
Eligibility engine: rules check each resident against every scheme by age, income, land and documents, and rank them: surest money first, fewest steps first.
Document gap planner: shows exactly which document blocks each scheme and the doorstep way to fix it (for example, no bank account means book IPPB).
AI caseworker agent: reads a caretaker note in Telugu or English, updates the profile, drafts the Form 1 cover letter, the Prajavani petition and the Elderline call script, and schedules follow-ups. It uses only the checked scheme list and never gives medical or legal advice.
Evidence timeline: every step saved with date, office, acknowledgement or grievance number, and the officer's reply. This is the proof that you never got an answer, and it powers escalation.
Escalation ladder: if no reply in a set number of days, the agent suggests the next free channel (ward office, then mandal Prajavani, then Elderline 14567, then district Collector) and drafts the message.
Share card: big-letter Telugu and English card on screen and as an image for WhatsApp.
Impact dashboard: residents screened, eligible, documents fixed, accounts opened, applications filed with numbers, escalations raised, replies received, money started, eye checks, medicine savings.
Later list (not now): pickles and sweets ledger, second home, voice calls.
5. Data: why we still need the chairman's information
Public demo and GitHub repo: made-up sample residents only. Anyone can see it.
Private pilot: real residents, with each person's consent, in a separate private database behind a staff login. Only Vasavi and two staff can see it. Never on GitHub.
What judges see: only totals, like "31 of 40 residents eligible for Aasara; 9 applications filed with acknowledgement numbers". No names.
So yes, the chairman's real information is needed. It runs the private pilot, and its totals are your proven impact.
