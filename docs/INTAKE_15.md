Removed from v1 and why:
- "Who can we call for you?": she lives in the home, so it does not fit. Moved to staff questions.
- "Applied and got no answer?": too hard to remember and feels like blame. Replaced by Q12 (softer).
- "Family land / pension?": awkward to ask an elder. Moved to staff questions.

## Part B: staff answer from records (not asked to the resident)
1. Family contact (if any) and how often they visit.
2. Family land, house, vehicle or pension (if known).
3. Monthly medicine bill for this resident.
4. Papers in the office: Aadhaar copy, ration card, receipts, old pension forms.
5. Last doctor or hospital visit.

## The card ("1 - Sarojini")
One card per resident, inside her home's space. Each card has:
- **Profile**: the answers above, with the date asked.
- **This month vs last month**: what changed (pension filed, glasses got, mood).
- **Notes with date**: "Last 3 days Sarojini is dull." Anyone in staff can add. Show to the right person.
- **Health**: problems, medicines, photo of prescription read by AI (staff confirms), hospital visits.
- **Bills**: medicine cost, hospital cost, money needed (surgery, glasses).
- **Places**: nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendra (for the home's PIN), LVPEI@Home with "what to say on the call".
- **Government steps**: scheme, missing document, form filed, acknowledgement number, reply, next escalation.
- **Download**: this card as PDF, or all cards as one Excel sheet.

## Many homes
- Each home has its own space. One home never sees another home's cards.
- Demo home: fake residents, no sign-in, for judges.
- Real homes: one 4-digit home PIN per phone.
